All the Bowling Fun in Hopewell, VA!

Hopewell Bowling Center is a family-owned Bowling Center located in Hopewell, VA, that has been serving generations of families for 70 years. Here, we provide an affordable and fun experience for you and everyone to enjoy.

Kids Bowl Free is currently active Sunday through Friday, where only kids can bowl for free for one game per visit.
