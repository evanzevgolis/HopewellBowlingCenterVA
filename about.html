About the Hopewell Bowling Center.

The Hopewell Bowling Center first opened in 1955 by Michael Zevgolis and a group of investors to be an affordable and fun bowling experience for families within the community for over 70 years.

The center is currently owned and operated by the Zevgolis Family. When first opened pin boys were assigned to pick up the pins until machines were installed, with the current machines replacing them in 1972.

In 1996, Michael added scoring systems which were the most advanced within the period AFter his passing in 2012, there was an outpour of support from the community to keep the center open.

In 2020, the center was remodeled and refirbished with new tables, chairs, flooring, air conditioning and a new roof. We also provide a snack-bar, birthday party packages, Open bowling, Cosmic Bowling, and Bowling for leagues.

Thank you for visiting our Website! and we look forward to serving you.
