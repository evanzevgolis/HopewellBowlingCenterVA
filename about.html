About the Hopewell Bowling Center.

The Hopewell Bowling Center first opened in 1955 by Michael Zevgolis to be an affordable and fun bowling experience for families within the community for over 75 years.

Kids Bowl Free is currently active Sunday through Friday, where only kids can bowl for free for one game per visit.
